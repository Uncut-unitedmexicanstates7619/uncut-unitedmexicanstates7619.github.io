❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which devices are supported?

omdrop-plugin works on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) running Omarchy. It can communicate with iPhones, iPads, and other Macs that support AirDrop.

Do I need an internet connection?

No! omdrop-plugin creates a local connection between your devices, so no internet is required. As long as your devices are nearby with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled, you are good to go.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit. However, very large files may take longer to transfer depending on your network speed. We recommend transferring files under 5GB for the best experience.

How do I update omdrop-plugin?

The application will automatically check for updates when you open it. Simply accept the update prompt when it appears to get the latest features and fixes.
